Privacy Policy

Spell Swarm: Arcade Shooter (Android) · Effective 22 October 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Spell Swarm: Arcade Shooter ("the game") collects. The game is published by MCG (M.C.G Consulting, a licensed business registered in Israel), referred to below as "we" or "us".

The short version: we do not collect any personal information. The game is in early testing and does not send your data to us.

What stays on your device

Your game progress and settings are saved only on your own device. We cannot see them, and they are deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

What we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The game has no accounts and no chat, and it does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts or photos. It contains no advertising and no analytics service run by us.

Technical information sent by the game engine

The game is built with the Unity engine. Unity Technologies may receive limited, anonymous technical information about the device the game runs on (for example the device model, operating system version and graphics hardware), which it uses to improve its engine. It does not identify you. Unity's handling of this information is described in the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

Google Play

If you install the game from Google Play, Google processes information about your download and your device under its own Privacy Policy. We do not receive personal information from Google about you.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

The game is still being made. If a future version starts to collect any information (for example to show optional ads that you choose to watch for a reward), we will update this policy before that version is released, describe exactly what is collected and why, and change the date above.
